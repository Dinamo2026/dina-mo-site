Accessibility statement

Dinamo wants everyone to be able to use this website comfortably. The site was built to follow WCAG 2.1 level AA and the Israeli standard IS 5568, as far as possible.

What the site provides

  • Layouts for phones, tablets and computers, and support for browser text zoom.
  • Full keyboard navigation with a clear focus outline, and a "Skip to content" link at the top of each page.
  • A "Pause motion" button that stops all videos, the rolling wheels, the place-name ticker and the photo gallery. The site remembers your choice.
  • Respect for your device's "reduce motion" setting.
  • Text alternatives for images, a clear heading structure and sufficient text contrast.
  • The videos are silent atmosphere clips with no speech.
