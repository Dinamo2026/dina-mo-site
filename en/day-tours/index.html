Day tours

Boutique tours, booked for groups

Boutique day tours built especially for your group: in the Elah Valley, riding through the vineyards with wine tastings, and in Gush Etzion, riding between wineries and springs.

What to expect

  • Elah Valley: riding through the vineyards with wine tastings
  • Gush Etzion: between wineries and springs
  • A private tour built for your group
  • Pace, length and stops set by the group

Format
